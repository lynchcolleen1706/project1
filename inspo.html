Style and Inspiration

Home Gallery

I am largely inspired by nature and its patterns. I enjoy the intricacies of flowers, spiral patters, and wavy lines. I also draw inspiration from the people I love and creating pieces they can use and cherish. While I try to have a rough plan for my pieces, some of my favorites have been the product of having zero plan and just letting myself flow.

Below is a list of specific sources of inspiration I have used:

  • Lupine Flowers
  • Hydrangeas
  • Star patterns
  • Geckos
  • Dragonflies
  • Ladybugs
