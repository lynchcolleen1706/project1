About Me

Hi! My name is Colleen. I'm a junior Wildlife and Fisheries Biology major at UVM, and I'm from southern Maine. I started pottery 5 years ago in high school and currently make art in the UVM SoA Ceramics Co-op studio. The fact that pottery is so different from my academic work makes it a huge creative outlet for me. It allows me to relax and just exist without the pressure of school or work.
